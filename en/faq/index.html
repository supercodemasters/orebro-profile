Frequently asked questions

Answers to what businesses most often ask about meetings, cost, services and how a project works.

Are you a large consultancy?

No. You talk directly to me, the same person who designs and builds the solution.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

Rarely. Often it is enough to connect what you already have or build what is missing around it.

Do you speak Swedish?

I am learning Swedish and speak fluent English. Written material can be in Swedish.

Do you only work in Örebro?

No. I live in Örebro and work with clients all over Sweden. We always start with a short video or phone call.

What does a first meeting cost?

Nothing. We take 15 minutes on video or phone, and you get a short proposal afterwards.

Custom systems

How do I know if we need our own system?

If you spend a lot of time on manual entry, Excel and email even though you use standard software, a custom system or add-on can pay off. We work that out in a first meeting.

Can we start small?

Yes. We start with the part that saves the most time and build on it once it works day to day.

Booking systems

Isn’t a ready-made booking service enough?

Often it is. Your own booking flow is worth it only when you have special rules, several resources or need to link bookings to other systems.

Can customers get reminders?

Yes, reminders by email or text message can be sent automatically before the visit.

Integrations

Do we have to replace systems to integrate them?

Rarely. Most systems have an API or an export, so data can be moved between them automatically.

What if a system has no API?

Then import and export via CSV or Excel can often be automated instead.

Dashboards and reporting

Where do the numbers come from?

Directly from the systems you already use, such as the web shop, booking or accounting, so nobody has to paste data by hand.

Can reports be exported?

Yes, to Excel and PDF, for example for the board or the auditor.

ERP and finance

Does it replace our accounting software?

Not necessarily. It is often better to build purchasing and inventory around the accounting system you already have and connect them.

Why does accounting knowledge matter?

A business system must produce correct records for the bookkeeping. Masood holds an ACCA accounting technician qualification.

E-commerce and web shops

Can the web shop show the same stock as the shop?

Yes, with a sync between inventory and web shop, both show the same stock levels.

Which payment methods can be used?

Card, PayPal and other payment solutions, depending on what the web shop supports.

Shall we take 15 minutes?

Tell me what takes up unnecessary time. We start with a short video or phone call – free of charge.
