Services

A booking system adapted to your business

Ready-made booking services work well for many. When they are not enough – special rules, several resources or a link to the accounting system – your own booking flow can be worth it.

What a booking system can solve

  • Online booking around the clock, fewer phone calls
  • Reminders by email or text message
  • Planning of rooms, premises, staff or equipment
  • Payment at booking and records for the bookkeeping

Relevant experience

  • Patient booking with doctor availability and a link to medical records for a medical practice (Alpine Medical Associates, USA)
  • Real-time notifications and collaboration (ITG, Turkey)
  • Web shops with payments, and mobile payments (USA, Ghana)
  • Roles and permissions for many users

How it works

We meet for 15 minutes in Örebro or remotely. You get a short proposal, and we start small. I stay on for support afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Isn’t a ready-made booking service enough?

Often it is. Your own booking flow is worth it only when you have special rules, several resources or need to link bookings to other systems.

Can customers get reminders?

Yes, reminders by email or text message can be sent automatically before the visit.
