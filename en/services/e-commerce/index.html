Services

E-commerce and web shops

A web shop should not become an extra job. When shop, inventory and payments fit together, you avoid selling items you do not have and entering orders twice.

What it can cover

  • Web shop with checkout, gift cards and discount codes
  • Payment by card, PayPal and other methods
  • Stock levels and prices in sync with the warehouse
  • Order confirmations and delivery status by email

Experience

  • Health products marketplace with multiple sellers, invoices and shipment tracking (Alimila, Turkey)
  • Jewellery shop on Magento with one-page checkout and gift cards (59 Souls, USA)
  • Stock and price sync with Magento (PopLeads, Turkey)

How it works

We meet for 15 minutes in Örebro or remotely. You get a short proposal, and we start small. I stay on for support afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Can the web shop show the same stock as the shop?

Yes, with a sync between inventory and web shop, both show the same stock levels.

Which payment methods can be used?

Card, PayPal and other payment solutions, depending on what the web shop supports.
