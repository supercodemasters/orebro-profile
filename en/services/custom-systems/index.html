Services

Custom systems for small and medium-sized businesses

Off-the-shelf software often fits 80 percent. The rest ends up in Excel, email and manual work. A custom system is built around your own workflows instead.

Examples of what can be built

  • Admin tools for orders, cases or members
  • Customer and supplier portals with login and permissions
  • Document management with versions and export to PDF and Excel
  • Mobile-friendly web apps for staff in the field

Experience

  • Supplier portal for 500+ suppliers with roles and permissions (ITG, Turkey)
  • Case management system for a law firm (CFN Teknoloji, Turkey)
  • Project, staff and inventory management for a shipyard, with approval workflows and offline mode (Uzmar, Turkey)
  • Document and asset management in a SaaS for IT service companies (ITBoost, USA)

How I work

  • Requirements and a feasibility study before anything is built
  • A clear quote and delivery plan
  • A test and review environment before go-live

How it works

We meet for 15 minutes in Örebro or remotely. You get a short proposal, and we start small. I stay on for support afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if we need our own system?

If you spend a lot of time on manual entry, Excel and email even though you use standard software, a custom system or add-on can pay off. We work that out in a first meeting.

Can we start small?

Yes. We start with the part that saves the most time and build on it once it works day to day.
