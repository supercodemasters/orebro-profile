Dashboards and reporting

When the numbers live in several systems, every monthly report becomes a puzzle. A dashboard fetches the data automatically and shows what actually drives the business.

Examples

  • Sales per day, week and product
  • Occupancy, bookings and cancellations
  • Stock levels and purchasing needs
  • Export to Excel and PDF for the board and auditor

Experience

  • Finance dashboard in an ERP system (Samsara, Bulgaria)
  • Dashboards for purchasing and suppliers (ITG, Turkey)
  • Consent statistics with export to PDF and Excel
  • Google Analytics and Mixpanel dashboards (USA)
  • Reports with IBM Cognos BI

How it works

We meet for 15 minutes in Örebro or remotely. You get a short proposal, and we start small. I stay on for support afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the numbers come from?

Directly from the systems you already use, such as the web shop, booking or accounting, so nobody has to paste data by hand.

Can reports be exported?

Yes, to Excel and PDF, for example for the board or the auditor.
